Super Flow is a free, open-source dictation app and meeting Notetaker for macOS. Speech recognition, cleanup, speaker labels and notes run on local models. Nothing leaves your Mac.

What it does

Dictation into any app

Hold Fn for push-to-talk, Fn+Space or a double tap for hands-free with live text. A 300 ms pre-roll and 150 ms post-roll keep every syllable.

Cleanup that keeps your words

Fillers, false starts and "no wait, Thursday" handled; numbers and lists written out; a style per app. The local model is checked by guardrails and the rules-only text wins whenever it strays.

Dictionary and snippets

Names and spellings the engines must keep, replacement rules, words learned from your edits, and spoken triggers that expand to longer text.

Command Mode and Transforms

Fn+Control with text selected: say what to do with it. ⌥1 polishes a selection; ⌥2, ⌥3 run your own prompts.

Meeting Notetaker

"Meeting detected — Record?" when a call holds your mic. You and them on separate tracks, a live transcript, speakers, notes with decisions and action items, Markdown export. Audio deleted after 30 days.

The Flow Bar

A small pill at the bottom of the screen shows every state, "Getting ready… 40%" while a model downloads, and any failure as a card with the fix.

Privacy

Audio, transcripts, notes and settings stay in ~/Library/Application Support/Super Flow. The app makes exactly two kinds of connection, each with its own switch: model downloads from Hugging Face (automatic after install, unmetered networks only) and an anonymous update check against GitHub Releases. Turn both off and it never connects. Password fields are never read or dictated into; a meeting is never recorded without a click. The full data inventory.

Install

  1. Run the one-liner above, or download the DMG and drag Super Flow to Applications.
  2. The app is signed with the project's own identity, not notarized. If macOS says it "cannot be opened", open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. The installer handles this for you.
  3. Grant Microphone and Accessibility when asked; the Notetaker asks for System Audio Recording the first time you record a call. Each permission is explained on its own screen.
  4. Hold Fn and speak. The speech model downloads first; the bar says "Getting ready…" until it is there.

Uninstall: curl -fsSL https://udsy19.github.io/super-flow/install.sh | bash -s -- --uninstall
